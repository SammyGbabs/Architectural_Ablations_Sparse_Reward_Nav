%%%% NeurIPS paper checklist. Answers reflect the submitted version only.
%%%% Anonymity: no answer may name an author, institution, or repository.

\begin{enumerate}

\item {\bf Claims}\\
Do the main claims made in the abstract and introduction accurately reflect the paper's
contributions and scope?
\answerYes{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: The abstract and introduction claim exactly two things, a reusable
  pre-registered protocol and a worked negative outcome, and both are delivered in
  \S\ref{sec:protocol} and \S\ref{sec:results}. The abstract states explicitly that the
  result is a statement about the task rather than evidence against the architecture
  hypothesis, which is the paper's central scope limit.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Limitations}\\
Does the paper discuss the limitations of the work performed by the authors?
\answerYes{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: \S\ref{sec:discussion} states the scope limit directly: we show this
  task class cannot test the hypothesis, and explicitly have \emph{not} shown that inverted
  asymmetry fails in reinforcement learning generally. \S\ref{sec:poscontrol} reports a
  pre-registered prediction (P3) that was wrong, and Appendix~\ref{app:mechanism} flags the
  memorisation account as an interpretation consistent with the data rather than a directly
  measured property. The positive control is on image classification, not RL, which is stated
  as a limitation rather than glossed.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Theory assumptions and proofs}\\
Does the paper provide the full set of assumptions and a complete (and correct) proof?
\answerNA{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: The paper is empirical and methodological; it contains no theoretical
  results or proofs.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Experimental result reproducibility}\\
Does the paper fully disclose all the information needed to reproduce the main experimental
results?
\answerYes{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: Appendix~\ref{app:repro} describes the full pipeline. All
  hyperparameters live in configuration files included in the supplementary material, the
  environment and observation-wrapper code is included, training is seeded end-to-end, and the
  bootstrap resampling seed is fixed so that every reported confidence interval reproduces to
  the digit from the released per-seed CSVs.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Open access to data and code}\\
Does the paper provide open access to the data and code, with sufficient instructions to
faithfully reproduce the main experimental results?
\answerYes{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: An anonymised supplementary archive accompanies the submission
  containing the per-seed CSVs, the analysis and plotting scripts, the environment and wrapper
  code, the configuration files, and the frozen pre-registration documents, with reproduction
  commands in its README. A public repository link will be added in the camera-ready version.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Experimental setting/details}\\
Does the paper specify all the training and test details necessary to understand the results?
\answerYes{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: \S\ref{sec:phase1} gives the environment, action space, observation
  dimension, reward function, and step budget. Appendix~\ref{app:phase1} gives the full
  configuration table with per-configuration network architectures and the varied
  hyperparameters. Appendix~\ref{app:phase2} gives the ladder mechanisms and their parameters;
  every remaining hyperparameter is in the supplementary configuration files.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Experiment statistical significance}\\
Does the paper report error bars suitably and correctly defined or other appropriate
information about the statistical significance of the experiments?
\answerYes{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: Every aggregate is an interquartile mean with a 95\% stratified
  bootstrap confidence interval over 10 seeds, computed with rliable. The aggregation
  conventions, including the seed-mean definition for per-room success rates and the exclusion
  of a sentinel value from the sample-efficiency statistic, are stated in
  Appendix~\ref{app:phase1}. Single-seed results are used only as learnability gates and are
  never reported as findings, a distinction the paper states explicitly and enforces.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Experiments compute resources}\\
Does the paper provide sufficient information on the computer resources needed to reproduce
the experiments?
\answerYes{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: All runs were executed serially on a single commodity cloud GPU
  instance. Each Phase 1 configuration is 10 seeds at 200k environment steps; the Phase 2
  ladder adds six 10-seed cells at 200k or 500k steps. The positive control is CPU-feasible at
  10 seeds and a two-epoch budget. Per-run step budgets are given in
  Table~\ref{tab:ladder} and Appendix~\ref{app:phase1}.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Code of ethics}\\
Does the research conducted in the paper conform, in every respect, with the NeurIPS Code of
Ethics?
\answerYes{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: The work uses a synthetic grid-world environment and MNIST. It involves
  no human subjects, no personal data, and no deployed system.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Broader impacts}\\
Does the paper discuss both potential positive societal impacts and negative societal impacts
of the work performed?
\answerNA{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: The contribution is a methodological check on whether a benchmark can
  test a hypothesis. Its foreseeable effect is on evaluation practice within machine learning
  research, and we identify no direct societal impact, positive or negative, beyond improved
  reliability of published empirical claims.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Safeguards}\\
Does the paper describe safeguards that have been put in place for responsible release of data
or models that have a high risk for misuse?
\answerNA{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: The released artifacts are small grid-world policies, analysis scripts,
  and per-seed result tables. They carry no misuse risk.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Licenses for existing assets}\\
Are the creators or original owners of assets used in the paper properly credited and are the
license and terms of use explicitly mentioned and properly respected?
\answerYes{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: The paper cites the rliable evaluation methodology, the PPO algorithm,
  and MNIST-based experimental design, and the supplementary material lists all software
  dependencies with their versions. All are standard open-source research assets used in
  accordance with their licenses.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf New assets}\\
Are new assets introduced in the paper well documented and is the documentation provided
alongside the assets?
\answerYes{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: The supplementary archive documents the environment, the four
  observation-degradation wrappers, the analysis scripts, and the per-seed data, with a README
  giving the layout, a data-to-claim map linking each paper element to the CSV and script that
  produce it, and reproduction commands.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Crowdsourcing and research with human subjects}\\
Does the paper include the full text of instructions given to participants and screenshots, if
applicable, as well as details about compensation?
\answerNA{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: The paper involves no crowdsourcing and no human subjects.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Institutional review board (IRB) approvals}\\
Does the paper describe potential risks incurred by study participants, disclose whether such
risks were disclosed to the subjects, and obtain IRB approvals?
\answerNA{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: The paper involves no human subjects, so no IRB review was required.
\end{itemize}

\item {\bf Declaration of LLM usage}\\
Does the paper describe the usage of LLMs if it is an important, original, or non-standard
component of the core methods in this research?
\answerNA{}
\begin{itemize}
\item[] Justification: No large language model forms any part of the core method. The
  contribution is an experimental protocol and its instantiation on reinforcement-learning and
  image-classification tasks.
\end{itemize}

\end{enumerate}
